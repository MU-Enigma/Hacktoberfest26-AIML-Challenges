# SIMILARITY

OK. First of all, What is **similarity** in this context?  Imagine there are two action films. Now Categorise this action movie into different elements within it like Action Sequences, No. of Songs and Duration of the film. For Instance, Film A has 8 action sequences, 6 songs and its duration is 154 minutes. Consecutively, Film B has 6 action sequences, 5 songs and its duration is 143 minutes. If you just look at the stats, the  Film B seems completely different because it has way more of everything. Similarity ignores the total runtime and looks at the recipe of the movie. It notices that both films share the exact same ratio: two action scenes for every one song. Because their core "vibe" points in the exact same direction, the algorithm recognizes them as a perfect match in style. If we talk about the song spotlight, it just finds the track that shares the same vibe through some given parameters and then gives our desired output.

# SQUASHING
Squashing functions are nothing but just forcing numbers into a strict restricted boundary. (Just like **CHIRU BOSS** Thokkeyadam )
So, There are three types of squashing numbers (I think)
**Sigmoid**: It just limits between 0 and 1
**Tanh**: Same as Sigmoid, but limits between -1 and 1
**ReLU**: It doesnt like negative numbers and changes it to 0. But lets positive numbers go as high as it wants to.
