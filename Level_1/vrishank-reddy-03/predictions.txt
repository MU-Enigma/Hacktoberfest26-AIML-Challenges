Pick: Fresher Night
Runner-up: Canteen Bass

Why I picked it:
I treated tempo and duration as the columns that make two songs feel alike
when you play them back to back. Fresher Night is only 4 BPM away from my
loved song (128 vs 124) and 22 seconds away on duration (232 vs 210). It is
also high on energy (0.90 vs 0.78) and danceability (0.88 vs 0.82), so the
overall feel matches too.

Where it is weakest:
Energy is off by 0.12, which is the biggest gap on the feel columns. I
accepted that because it is still a high-energy, danceable track, just a bit
more intense.

Why my runner-up lost:
Canteen Bass is closer on energy (0.04 off) and danceability (0.03 off), but
it is 8 BPM and 36 seconds away on tempo and duration. Since I weighted tempo
and duration more, it came second.

Column I mostly ignored:
None completely, but I gave energy and danceability less weight than tempo,
because their gaps are small numbers and I assumed they matter less.
