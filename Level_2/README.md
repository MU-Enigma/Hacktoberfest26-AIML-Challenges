# Level 2: Learning by Gradient Descent

Put your work in **`Level_2/<your-github-username>/`**. Full task description is in the [main README](../README.md#level-2-learning-by-gradient-descent-oct-7-workshop).

## What to submit

- Your logistic regression code (you may reuse your own Level 1 code)
- Accuracy on the **test** set from a split you made yourself (fixed random seed), and your convergence criterion
- Loss curves for three learning rates, plus your predictions for each before you ran them
- `analysis.md`: your error analysis (3 examples the model gets wrong, one hypothesis, one test of it)
- A short **Jupyter notebook that teaches** your model step by step to someone who has never seen it

## Data

`datasets/binary_classification.csv`. The columns are described in [`datasets/README.md`](../datasets/README.md).
