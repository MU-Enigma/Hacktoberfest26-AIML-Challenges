The river runs past the old mill and the river carries the leaves of autumn.
In the morning the mill is quiet and the river is cold.
In the evening the lights of the town shine on the water and the water shines back.
A small boat waits by the mill. The boat is red and the boat is old.
Every day the old man walks to the river and every day he sits by the boat.
He watches the leaves float past the mill and he watches the lights of the town.
The town is small and the town is kind. The people of the town know the old man.
They bring him bread in the morning and they bring him tea in the evening.
When the river is high the boat stays by the mill, and when the river is low the boat goes out.
The old man says the river remembers everything that floats on it.
The children of the town do not believe him, but they listen anyway.
